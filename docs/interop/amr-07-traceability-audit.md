# amr-07 traceability audit (INTEROP-2a)

**Date:** 2026-10-28. **Checkout audited:** `main` at `3946b14`. **Decisions:** proposed in
[decision record 0011](../../spec/decisions/0011-interop-2a-bundle-errata.md).

Every behavior of the reference amr-07 that shows on the wire, in receipts, in claims (binding
`event` frames) or in timing, traced to the published specifications that will form the gate 2
bundle: RFC-001, Core 0.1 with its schemas and fixtures, reference simulation 0.1, the gate 2
specification and decision records 0007-0010. An independent author reads only these
(0009, I1, I3), so a rule that is not traceable is a question or a finding waiting to happen.

**Result:** 144 rows: 59 TRACED, 67 AMBIGUOUS, 18 MISSING. The candidate themes at the end
became decisions N1-N14 and reference defects D1-D7 of decision record 0011. This document
is reviewer evidence, not part of the bundle: it cites reference code (`src/`), which the
independent author must not read.

Legend. Reference files: **P** = `src/robolang/arch02/participants.py`, **E** = `engine.py`, **V** = `evidence.py`, **X** = `src/robolang/interop/participant.py`, **W** = `interop/view.py`, **H** = `interop/harness.py`, **R** = `src/robolang/core/receiver.py`. Spec files: **Core** = `spec/core-v0.1.md`, **Sim** = `spec/reference-simulation-v0.1.md`, **G2** = `spec/interop-gate-2-v0.1.md`, **RFC** = rfc-001, **D07..D10** = decision records. Classes: TRACED (spec text fixes what an independent author needs), AMBIGUOUS (spec allows more than one behavior or does not fix a value/order/format), MISSING (no spec text).
Impact tags in the resolution column: [HARNESS] the reference harness would fail or flag an independent run that differs (BindingFailure/InconsistentClaim, or receipt/send-sequence mismatch under G2 9.1); [JCS] only JCS identity or run-record identity differs (G2 9.2); [LATENT] not reachable in the 24 cases.

Known findings (listed, not re-analyzed): **E2** view lists sensors as "name and scope" but observations need `valid_for`, `confidence`, `method` (G2 6.4 vs Core 5.6; rows M24, S2). **E3** who sends the reply again to a duplicate (rows R10). **E5** hold timers of a silent participant are owned by amr-07 and skipped while silent (row R16). E1 and E4 are already resolved by D10.

Section 4.3 of Sim is cited for the observation ID. The Sim sections 6 to 8 are the ones G2 section 3 says define amr-07's required behavior; Sim 5 (messages) is **not** in that list, which matters for M1 and M4.
