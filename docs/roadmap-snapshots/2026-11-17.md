## Findings

- None of the 13 near sub-activities is fully expressible in RoboLang 1.6; each needs 3–4 gaps.
- Repetition (G-LOOP, out of scope since N4) is needed by 11 of 13 near and 21 of 38 sub-activities: the
  recommended design is a mission layer above tasks (2.0), after its own decision record (LE-9).
- Perception (G-PERCEIVE, 19 sub-activities) and run-time choice (G-CHOOSE, 17) carry the highest language need.
